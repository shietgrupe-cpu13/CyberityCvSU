Risk treatment guide The four things you can do with any risk expand
MITIGATE add a control that lowers likelihood or impact TRANSFER shift the cost of the damage to someone else under contract (e.g. insurance) AVOID stop doing the risky activity entirely, so the risk no longer exists ACCEPT knowingly live with it: the risk owner signs off and it is reviewed next cycle
Every control on this page is a form of mitigation.
